How Kordy Works

  1. 1Initial inquiry  Share your goals, symptoms, or treatment interest.
  2. 2Medical information review  We review available records and care needs.
  3. 3Hospital matching  Appropriate hospitals or clinics are suggested.
  4. 4Appointment planning  Scheduling, document support, and preparation.
  5. 5Treatment in Korea  Guided access to your selected medical pathway.
  6. 6Follow-up support  Post-visit communication and next-step guidance.

The final hospital and treatment pathway depend on individual medical needs and provider review.
